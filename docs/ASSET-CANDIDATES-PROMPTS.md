# 새 에셋 후보 생성 기록

2026-09-09. ChatGPT 내장 `image_gen` 도구 사용. CLI/API는 사용하지 않았다. 도구에서 모델 버전 선택이나 확인을 제공하지 않아 요청한 Images 2.5 사용 여부를 확인할 수 없다. 현재 게임에 적용하지 않은 비교 후보이다.

## 아틀라스 후보 — 실제 프롬프트

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a new production material atlas candidate for City Pursuit, a browser 3D arcade city driving game. Square high resolution. Exactly four equal square quadrants at 50% width and height, no gutters, no frames, no labels. TOP LEFT: flat orthographic base color texture of smooth dark graphite asphalt, very fine restrained aggregate, subtle wear, uniform brightness, no markings, no puddles, no directional shadows. Designed for unobtrusive repetition behind bright game lane lines. TOP RIGHT: straight-on orthographic modern downtown facade texture, perfectly regular 6 by 6 grid of blue-grey glass windows with thin dark graphite architectural mullions, approximately one in four windows softly warm amber interior; restrained readable large shapes rather than clutter, grid continuing naturally to tile boundaries, absolutely no perspective. BOTTOM LEFT: uniformly teal automotive metallic paint base-color texture, extremely subtle fine metallic grain, no directional highlight or vignette, no body panels or cars, tileable flat material. BOTTOM RIGHT: high-quality stylized arcade game concept art, a teal unbranded muscular sports coupe viewed from rear three-quarter with black-and-white pursuing police sedans and ordinary traffic in a clearly readable gridded downtown street, warm dusk sky, cool blue buildings, strong vehicle silhouettes, restrained neon, no motion blur, clean stylized 3D aesthetic. First three quadrants must be flat texture maps and only fourth is scene concept art. No lettering, logos, watermarks, borders. This is a new alternative asset, not an alteration of an existing file.

## 체크포인트 데칼 — 실제 프롬프트

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent game ground decal candidate for City Pursuit, a top-down-readable checkpoint destination marker placed on asphalt in a browser 3D arcade driving game. Create ONE isolated square composition on a genuinely transparent alpha background. Perfect overhead orthographic view, no perspective. An elegant wide segmented amber-gold circular ring around an OPEN TRANSPARENT central area, with three bold upward-pointing golden chevrons inside the ring indicating forward travel. Arrowheads and ring must be solid clearly readable shapes with subtle brushed luminous paint surface, restrained edge glow only and crisp silhouettes, not thick smoke. Plenty of transparent padding around outer ring so no clipping. Cohesive premium dusk city racing aesthetic. This is an actual flat decal texture, not a screenshot or scene: no ground, no asphalt backdrop, no vehicle, no scenery, no people, no words, no numbers, no logo, no UI frame, no checkerboard drawn into image. Ring interior and all negative space truly transparent. Strong simple geometry readable at small game sizes.

## 검토

아틀라스는 네 사분면으로 생성되었고 도로, 창문, 도장과 컨셉 장면이 구분된다. 도로와 외벽 반복 경계는 실제 게임 적용 전에 추가 점검해야 한다. 오른쪽 아래 장면은 3D 차량 모델이 아닌 분위기 참고 이미지이며, 텍스처 교체만으로 차량 형태나 게임 장면이 그 그림처럼 변경되지는 않는다. 체크포인트 데칼은 게임에 추가 적용할 후보이며 새 기능을 구현한 결과가 아니다.
